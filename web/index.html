Podcast Filter

Subscribe to a part of a podcast feed. Point the service at an RSS feed, describe which episodes you want by their title, and get a URL your podcast client can subscribe to.

How it works

  1. You give a feed and a few patterns. Every pattern is a Go regular expression tested against the episode title.
  2. Keep patterns are alternatives. An episode is in as soon as it matches any one of them, so two patterns mean «either of these». With no keep pattern at all, every episode is in.
  3. Drop patterns are vetoes. An episode matching any drop pattern is out, even if a keep pattern wanted it. Use them to carve exceptions out of what the keep patterns let in.
  4. You subscribe to the resulting URL. The service fetches the original feed on every request, removes the episodes that did not survive, and serves the rest as a normal RSS feed.

Patterns use RE2 syntax, which has no look-around but does take inline flags: (?i) makes a pattern case-insensitive, so (?i)shulman matches Shulman and SHULMAN alike. Unicode works as is — (?i)(Шульман) is a perfectly good pattern.

Start from an existing URL

You can also open /?id=<link id> or /?feed=…&re=… directly. A filtered URL or a link id is loaded into the form below, so you can debug and change it. A plain feed URL just fills in the feed field and keeps your filters.
